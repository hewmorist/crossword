Daily use: take a screenshot of the puzzle and press Puzzle Graphic. Choose the image from Photos or Files. Enter the number of Rows and Columns in the grid (the normal daily NYT puzzle is 15 × 15; Sunday is typically 21 × 21), then press Set Grid Corners. Click the outside top-left and bottom-right corners of the grid. Tap/click a square and type. The current Across/Down answer is lightly highlighted; tap the selected square, press Space, or use the Across/Down button to change direction. Save Crossword creates one saved file containing the image, grid and your answers; Open Saved resumes it later. Resume Last restores the browser's automatic save after closing and reopening this page. The image and answers stay on your device.
